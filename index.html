What We Do

Screeding Services Across Munster

From new builds to renovations, we deliver a smooth, durable, perfectly level finish ready for your chosen floor covering.

💧

Liquid Screed

Fast-flowing, self-levelling liquid (anhydrite/calcium sulphate) screed for new builds and renovations. Pumped directly into place for a perfectly flat, crack-resistant finish with faster drying times than traditional sand-cement screed.

🔥

Underfloor Heating Screed Overlays

Liquid screed is the ideal partner for underfloor heating — it fully encapsulates the pipework, maximises thermal conductivity, and gives faster, more even heat-up times than sand-cement alternatives.

📏

Floor Levelling & Self-Levelling Compound

Correcting uneven, damaged or sloping subfloors before tiling, timber, vinyl or carpet installation. A perfectly flat base is essential for a professional, long-lasting floor finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Liquid screed (also called flowing or anhydrite screed) is a self-levelling floor screed pumped into place in liquid form. It flows around obstacles and pipework, sets to a perfectly flat surface, and typically dries faster than traditional sand-cement screed.

Drying times depend on screed depth, ventilation and site conditions, but liquid screed is generally walkable within 24-48 hours and ready for floor coverings sooner than traditional sand-cement screed. We'll confirm exact timelines for your project when we quote.

Yes — liquid screed is the preferred choice for underfloor heating. It fully surrounds the heating pipes with no air gaps, giving excellent heat transfer and more even, efficient heating throughout the floor.

Liquid screed is pumped and self-levels, giving a flatter finish, faster installation and better performance with underfloor heating. Traditional sand-cement screed is hand-laid and can take longer to dry. We can advise which is right for your project.

Yes — we carry out screeding projects across Cork, Limerick, Waterford, Tipperary, Kerry and Clare, for both domestic and commercial clients.
